# Camera, microphone, media and peer connections

**Status (2026-09-19, codec 1, Android): the camera and microphone work through `getUserMedia`.
WebRTC between phones is not measured yet.**

- **Camera:** `getUserMedia({ video: { facingMode: "environment" } })` works, and so does a still at
  480×640. FARE captured 12.3 KiB and compressed it to 3.2 KiB in 952 ms; the canvas re-encode
  strips EXIF.
- **QR codes:** `BarcodeDetector` is built in on Android (almanac P12). Where it's missing (iOS is
  unmeasured), a JavaScript reader is about 62 KiB (`qr` 0.7.0).
- **Microphone:** it opens. `MediaRecorder` supports 6 of 8 tested formats.
- **Device labels** are empty until a grant: 3 devices showed, 0 with labels.
- **Screen capture** is absent, as expected on mobile. **Picture-in-Picture** is disabled for the
  document.

## Peer connections (calls, live data)

`RTCPeerConnection` exists, and on 2026-09-18 it gathered ICE candidates. What is **not measured**:

- two phones connecting inside the app, after a `WebRtc` grant (almanac P13)
- two phones on different cellular networks, where carrier-grade NAT usually needs a TURN relay

The plan being built is Statement Store signalling with a minified, non-trickle SDP: one
statement per side, replaced in place. See [statement-store.md](statement-store.md) and
[sources/fare.md](../sources/fare.md).
